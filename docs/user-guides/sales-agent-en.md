# AKAI CRM — Sales Agent Quick Guide

This guide is written for field Sales Agents. Use the English/Urdu language switcher at any time. The same account and records remain available in both languages.

## Sign in

Open the AKAI CRM link, enter your email and password, and choose **Log in**. If you cannot sign in, use **Forgot password**. Never share your password. After sign-in, the system opens your Sales portal. You should see only the customers assigned to you or your permitted team.

## Today

Start on **Today**. The top cards show your Karachi-business-day work. Open a follow-up to call, open WhatsApp, or mark it done. Customer phone numbers are ready to tap. If the customer is not listed, use **Customers** search rather than creating a duplicate.

## Find a customer

Open **Customers** and search by shop name, phone, or area. Open one customer to see contact details, orders, quotes, activities, ledger information available to your role, and follow-ups. Daniyal’s customers must not appear in Haris’s list, and vice versa.

## Enrich a customer

Open **Customer enrichment**. Work on one shop at a time, complete the phone, WhatsApp, contact person, address, and customer type, then choose **Save and next**. If WhatsApp is the same as the primary phone, tick the checkbox. Tap **Capture location** only when the shop agrees and you are physically there. Location is never captured in the background.

## Log a call or activity

Tap the activity button and choose Call, WhatsApp, Email, Visit, Note, or Meeting. Write a short useful note. For a callback, select a follow-up date. When you return to the app after using a phone `tel:` link, the call sheet appears so you can record duration, result, notes, and the next follow-up.

## Place an order for a customer

Open **New order**, select the customer first, then choose products from that customer’s permitted catalogue. Review quantity, price, payment method, delivery note, and approval warning. Use **Place order** only after the customer confirms. If the device is offline, the order is visibly queued and will sync when the connection returns. Do not press the action repeatedly.

## Record a collection

Open **Recovery** and choose the customer. Enter the exact amount and payment method, add the reference or note, and attach evidence if required. Tap **Record collection**. A collection does not reduce the balance until the relevant deposit/payment verification rule is completed. A cheque changes the balance only after it is cleared.

## Quotes

Open **Quotes** to see requests assigned to you. Price each requested line carefully, set the validity date, and choose **Publish quote**. The vendor must explicitly accept. A later price-list change must not change an open quoted price during its validity period.

## Offline work

The app can show cached customer data and permitted catalogue data. Queued writes show a count such as “3 items waiting to sync”. Reconnect before leaving the area and wait for the sync result. Conflict handling is last-write-wins for the supported offline operations; review any error instead of assuming that a failed item was saved.

## Urdu language

Choose Urdu from the language switcher. The page becomes right-to-left: navigation, back controls, table order, and directional icons mirror. Prices, phone numbers, dates, SKUs, and quantities remain Western numerals and read left-to-right inside isolated fields.

## If something goes wrong

Read the message beneath the action. It explains what happened and the next step. If an order, collection, or sync item fails, take a screenshot, note the customer and time, and contact the Admin. Do not retry a financial action repeatedly without checking whether it was already recorded.

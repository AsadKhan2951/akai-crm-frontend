# AKAI CRM — Vendor Quick Guide

This guide is for AKAI dealer users. The screen is designed for a phone and supports English and Urdu.

## Sign in

Open the AKAI CRM link, enter your email and password, and choose **Log in**. Use **Forgot password** if needed. Do not share your password. After login, you should see only your own business account, balance, points, orders, quotes, and permitted catalogue.

## Browse the catalogue

From the home screen choose **Place order** or open **Catalogue**. Start with a Category or Brand, or search by product name or SKU. Category, Brand, and Collection are separate filters. A product that is visible in one dealer account is not automatically visible in another. If a product needs a quote, the screen says **Quote required** instead of showing an order price.

![Vendor catalogue screen](../vendor-flow-en-catalogue.png)

## Add products to cart

Open a product, choose the quantity, and tap **Add to cart**. Review the cart before checkout. If a price changed while the cart was open, the cart shows a notice and requires you to review it. It must not silently charge a new price.

## Place an order

Open **Cart**, check every line, quantity, price, total, payment method, and delivery note. Choose **Balance** or **Credit** as shown by your account. Read any approval or credit warning. Tap **Place order** once and wait for the result. The order confirmation shows the historical price recorded at the time of order.

![Vendor cart screen](../vendor-flow-en-cart.png)

## My orders

Open **My orders** to search and review order status. An order price remains the same even if the current price list changes. If the order needs Admin approval, wait for the approval result rather than placing the same order again.

## Request and accept a quote

For a quote-only product or a special quantity, choose **Request quote**. When the Sales Agent prices the quote, open **Quotes**, review each line and its validity date, and choose **Accept quote** only if correct. Acceptance can create the linked Order. An expired quote cannot be accepted without a new price.

![Vendor quote screen](../vendor-flow-en-quote.png)

## Balance and points

Open **My balance** for your permitted account balance and **My points** for loyalty points. A collection or order may require internal verification before the balance changes. Do not treat a pending collection as a cleared payment.

## Catalogue PDF and sharing

Use the catalogue PDF action when available. The PDF is customer-scoped and may be cached for a limited time. **Share on WhatsApp** opens a user-confirmed share action; AKAI CRM does not silently send a message from your account.

## Urdu language

Choose Urdu from the language switcher. The full page mirrors right-to-left, including navigation, back controls, table columns, and directional icons. Prices, SKUs, phone numbers, dates, and quantities remain Western numerals and read left-to-right.

## Offline and errors

If the phone loses internet, the app may show cached screens and will clearly identify supported queued work. Wait for sync when the connection returns. Read error messages and contact AKAI Admin if an order or quote fails. Do not tap **Place order** repeatedly without checking **My orders**.
